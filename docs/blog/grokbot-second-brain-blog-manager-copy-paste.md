<h2>Second brain: Notion vs Obsidian (not a throwaway .md file)</h2>

<p>Your grokbot is only as good as the context it re-reads. A single <code>app-brief.md</code> on the cloud filesystem is easy to lose across resets, duplicate across routines, or forget to update. A <strong>second brain</strong> in Notion or Obsidian gives you one canonical source the Bot must sync on every job.</p>

<p><strong>Notion</strong> works well when non-technical teammates edit voice or off-limits. Create a page titled <code>App Brain</code>. Use fixed sections: one-liner, audience, magic moment, pain bank, proof, voice, off-limits, what works for us, OpenQuok house rules, OpenQuok log, Winners, Failures. Add a simple table for post logs if you want sortable history.</p>

<p><strong>Obsidian</strong> works well when you already live in a vault and want local-first notes. Create <code>App-Brain.md</code> with the same headings. If you wire a Grok Bot ↔ Obsidian connector, point routines at that note only—do not scatter logs across daily notes unless you automate merges. For connector patterns, see the <a href="https://parazettel.com/articles/grok-bot-obsidian-connector/">Grok Bot Obsidian connector</a> walkthrough.</p>

<p>Whichever tool you pick, treat the brain like a product document. Version changes in chat are not enough. The Bot should show you diffs before it overwrites off-limits or house rules.</p>

<h2>Add Virlo for the daily trend loop</h2>

<p>Every morning grokbot should ask one question: is there an <em>honest</em> bridge from today's trend to our app? Most trends will fail that test. That is healthy. A forced tie-in hurts more than silence.</p>

<p><strong>X</strong> is the easy layer—Grok Bot can search it directly. <strong>TikTok</strong> and <strong>Instagram</strong> drive a large share of consumer discovery. Raw X signal does not replace short-form ranking. <strong>Virlo</strong> closes that gap—see <a href="https://virlo.ai/grokbot">Virlo grokbot</a>.</p>

<ol>
<li>Open Grok Bot <strong>Settings → Plugins</strong> and add Virlo, or follow Virlo's grokbot setup page.</li>
<li>Start with five hashtag terms until you see volume and cost.</li>
<li>Rank by save rate where available. When saves are missing, use comments divided by views.</li>
<li>Cap politics, tragedies, and anything in the brain's off-limits list before you score trends.</li>
</ol>

<p>Prompt 2 merges morning scan and multi-platform draft creation so you run one daily routine instead of two paste steps.</p>

<h2>Prompt 1 — Foundation (run once)</h2>

<p>Prompt 1 combines the <strong>app brain interview</strong> and <strong>OpenQuok house rules</strong>. Budget about an hour. Use voice dictation if that helps you brain-dump. Cover what the product does, who it is for, the magic moment, complaints, proof, and your ten best posts.</p>

<p>Every later prompt reads this brain. The trend scan scores against the pain bank and magic moment. The writer uses the voice section. Off-limits is your brand safety filter. The OpenQuok log in the brain replaces scattered log files so Monday reports can match hooks to outcomes.</p>

<p>Three house rules carry the system:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Draft-only publishing</strong> keeps you in control.</li>
<li><strong>TikTok inbox / UPLOAD</strong> when you need trending audio—finish in the app in about a minute. See <a href="/channels/tiktok">TikTok on OpenQuok</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Logging in the brain</strong> makes learning possible.</li>
</ul>

<p>Paste this into a new Grok Bot chat (run once):</p>
<pre><code>You are the social media manager for [APP NAME].

Interview me until you can explain the product to a stranger. Ask follow-ups about audience, magic moment, complaints, and proof.

Then update [NOTION PAGE URL or OBSIDIAN NOTE App-Brain.md] with:
- One-sentence pitch a 12-year-old would get
- Audience and what they already tried
- Magic moment (3 seconds where someone "gets it")
- Pain bank: 20 user problems in their words
- Proof: numbers, reviews, results I gave you
- Voice and off-limits
- "What works for us" from these best posts: [LINKS] — hook, format, length each

Also add a section "OpenQuok house rules" with these permanent rules:
1. Every openquok post is a DRAFT until I reply "approved" on that batch.
2. Convert my times to UTC before schedule.
3. Run openquok integration:schema before each network; respect limits.
4. Label AI and paid content per platform rules.
5. TikTok: use inbox / UPLOAD path when I need trending sound; I finish in the TikTok app.
6. Never duplicate captions across platforms—adapt each network.
7. Log every draft under "OpenQuok log" in this brain (date, platform, hook, trend source)—not a loose file on disk.

Mirror house rules in your long-term memory. The Notion page or Obsidian note is the source of truth.</code></pre>

<h2>Prompt 2 — Daily create (routine: 7am)</h2>

<p>Prompt 2 is your production engine. It reads off-limits, pulls X plus Virlo signal, scores honest bridges, sends you five candidates, waits for your picks, then drafts a multi-platform set as OpenQuok drafts with post IDs in the summary.</p>

<p>Platform shape still matters. X stays short with the hook on line one and no link in the body. LinkedIn gets a story arc. TikTok and Instagram get a six-slide script with the product on slides three or four. Threads rewrites X without copy-paste duplication.</p>

<p>After the routine fires, you make the <strong>brand safety</strong> call. Kill forced bridges.</p>

<p>Save this as a <strong>daily 7am</strong> routine:</p>
<pre><code>Every day at 7am:

1. Read the App Brain off-limits list.
2. Trends — X: last 24h high-engagement search in [YOUR NICHE] (min_faves:500 or higher in noisy topics). TikTok + Instagram: use Virlo on [HASHTAGS / SEARCH TERMS]; top 20 from last 3 days; rank by save rate (or comments/views if saves missing). Note format and sound.
3. Score each trend 1–10 on growth, honest tie to pain bank or magic moment, and ease to ship today. Drop forced bridges.
4. Send me top 5 with platform, link, why it moves, and one-sentence bridge. Wait for me to pick 2–3.
5. For each trend I picked, draft one post per connected platform:
   - X: short, hook first line, no link in body
   - LinkedIn: story, one sentence per line, 100–200 words
   - TikTok + Instagram: 6-slide slideshow script (app on slide 3–4)
   - Threads: rewrite X, not a copy
Three hook options each; pick the best. Use voice from the brain. Create all as openquok drafts. Send batch summary with post IDs. Append to "OpenQuok log" in the brain.</code></pre>

<h2>Prompt 3 — Weekly calendar (after you reply "approved")</h2>

<p>Once you approve a batch, you should not drag cards by hand unless you want to. Prompt 3 fills seven days using OpenQuok analytics when you have history, caps per-platform volume, avoids back-to-back same-topic posts, and leaves one open slot per day for a breaking morning trend.</p>

<p>The open slot lets a same-day trend ship without bumping approved work. Sparse analytics? Try <a href="/tools/best-time-to-post">Best Time to Post</a> until thirty-day data exists. The Bot must show the week as a table before it schedules. You reply <strong>approved</strong> on that table too.</p>

<p>Run after you approve a batch:</p>
<pre><code>I approved the drafts in [BATCH NAME OR DATE]. Schedule them across the next 7 days.

- Use openquok analytics:platform for the last 30 days for best slots; if thin, use [DEFAULT TIMES].
- Max [X] posts per platform per day.
- No back-to-back posts on the same topic.
- Leave one open slot per day for a same-day trend from the 7am scan.

Show the full week as a table (platform, time UTC, hook, post ID). Do not schedule until I reply "approved" on this table.</code></pre>

<h2>Prompt 4 — Operate and learn (two routines)</h2>

<p>Prompt 4 splits into two schedules. Save the text twice if your Bot needs separate routines: <strong>hourly</strong> (or every two to three hours if usage is tight) for viral watch, and <strong>Monday 8am</strong> for the learning report.</p>

<p>Viral windows are short. When a post beats your thirty-day baseline by a wide margin, you want draft follow-ups ready—not a scramble the next afternoon. The Monday read turns flops into rules in the brain.</p>

<p><strong>Schedule A — viral watch:</strong></p>
<pre><code>Viral watch: Check openquok analytics for posts live in the last 48 hours. If any beat our 30-day average by 3×:
- Message me with post and numbers immediately.
- Confirm link comment / plug exists; draft one if not.
- Draft adapted versions for platforms missing—drafts only.
- Draft 3 same-structure hook variants.
- Append to "Winners" in the App Brain.
Nothing publishes without my approval.</code></pre>

<p><strong>Schedule B — Monday read:</strong></p>
<pre><code>Monday read: Pull last 7 days from openquok analytics:platform. Match rows to "OpenQuok log" in the brain. Pull conversions from [APP STORE / REVENUECAT / STRIPE].

Sort each post: high/low views × converting/not. Actions:
- High views + converting → 3 variations this week
- High views + not converting → keep hook, rework CTA
- Low views + converting → keep angle, test 3 hooks
- Low views + not converting → kill; log in "Failures"

Then tell me: top 3 patterns, best platform, one thing to stop, any rule to add to the brain. Show brain edits before you save.</code></pre>

<h3>Leak 1 — Nobody can find your app</h3>

<p>Every serious post should make the next step obvious. For mobile apps that is often a one-line first comment with the App Store link. For SaaS it may be signup or a lead magnet URL. OpenQuok plugs fire after publish on supported channels so you do not type the same comment by hand. You still approve plug copy when the Bot drafts it inside Prompt 4.</p>

<h3>Leak 2 — Your winner lives on one platform</h3>

<p>A post that pops on X already passed a hard test. Within twenty-four hours it should exist elsewhere—adapted, not duplicated. Grokbot drafts the set; OpenQuok publishes. LinkedIn might need a story layout. TikTok might need slideshow plus inbox upload.</p>

<h3>Leak 3 — You only post winners once</h3>

<p>Your followers saw it. The rest of the platform did not. Put evergreen winners on a repeat cadence—often thirty days. Pair repeats with fresh hooks from Prompt 2.</p>

<h3>Leak 4 — Founder and brand accounts sit idle</h3>

<p>If you run a founder account and a brand account, they should amplify each other. Cross-account repost plugs let one channel's best work surface on the other after publish. Setup: <a href="/docs/automations/cross-account-plugs">cross-account plugs</a>.</p>

<h3>Leak 5 — Nobody is watching when something takes off</h3>

<p>The first hours after a spike decide how much you capture. Hourly viral watch is the routine to prioritize if you must choose one automation beyond the 7am draft loop.</p>

<h2>The Monday read: views are not the scoreboard</h2>

<p>Views are useful. Conversions are the scoreboard. A post with huge reach and weak signups needs a new call to action—not more reach. A post with modest reach and strong trials may need new hooks, not a new product.</p>

<table>
<thead>
<tr><th></th><th>Converting</th><th>Not converting</th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>High views</td><td>Draft three variations this week</td><td>Keep hook; rework CTA</td></tr>
<tr><td>Low views</td><td>Keep angle; test three hooks</td><td>Kill; log in Failures</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<p>The <strong>Failures</strong> section in your second brain is the most valuable long-term asset. Every flop becomes a rule for the next batch. If you get downloads but nobody pays, fix onboarding or paywall before you scale posting.</p>

<p>Non-app products: point Prompt 4 at signups, trials, MRR, or qualified leads. The loop stays the same.</p>

<h2>Where you still need a human</h2>

<ul>
<li>The brain interview—only you know the magic moment.</li>
<li>Trend picking—you are the brand safety filter.</li>
<li>Approving every batch and every week table.</li>
<li>TikTok trending sound—inbox finish in the app for about sixty seconds per clip.</li>
<li>Real replies to real people in comments.</li>
<li>Kill vs scale after the Monday sort.</li>
</ul>

<p>Grokbot makes the machine easy to start. Consistency still wins on simple work repeated longer than competitors.</p>

<h2>Prompts that work between routines</h2>

<p>Keep ad-hoc chat short. State the action, the channel, and the time—the same pattern as the Grok Bot scheduling walkthrough:</p>
<blockquote><p>What performed best on my channels over the last 7 days? Draft a follow-up for my top channel next Tuesday 9am. Save as draft only.</p></blockquote>
<blockquote><p>Rewrite this note for X and Threads. Stay under each limit. Save drafts for Friday 2pm workspace time. Do not publish.</p></blockquote>

<h2>What this stack costs (plain terms)</h2>

<p>Three bills usually apply. <strong>Grok Bot</strong> sits on your xAI or bundled plan—check current Grok Bot eligibility before you commit to hourly routines. <strong>OpenQuok</strong> is your scheduler workspace; start on <a href="/pricing">Pricing</a> and connect only the channels you will actually draft to. <strong>Virlo</strong> is usage-based for short-form pulls; begin on a small hashtag set so cost stays predictable while you validate signal quality.</p>

<p>You do not need a separate scraper bill for basic X search inside Grok Bot. You do need Virlo—or an equivalent short-form source—if TikTok and Instagram are growth pillars. Skipping that layer pushes the Bot toward forced trend tie-ins on networks it cannot see well.</p>
