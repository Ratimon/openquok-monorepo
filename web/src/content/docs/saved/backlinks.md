## Open the Backlinks tab

> Your signed-in shortlist for sites you bookmarked on Build Backlinks.

<p>Go to <a href="/account/saved?tab=backlinks">Account → Saved</a> → <strong>Backlinks</strong> tab.</p>

![Account -> Saved - > Choose Tab](/docs/_assets/saved/overview-tabs.webp)

![Explore Backlinks](/docs/_assets/saved/browse-backlinks.webp)

Each row is a site you saved from the public <a href="/build-backlinks">Build Backlinks</a> catalog. To browse or bookmark new sites, see <a href="/docs/directory-submission/build-backlinks">Build Backlinks</a>.

<Callout type="note">
<p>Backlinks bookmarks are separate from listing bookmarks on <strong>Libs → Browse</strong>.</p>
</Callout>

## Reorder your priorities

On a paid Cloud plan, use **Move up** and **Move down** to rank sites for outreach.

![Manage Your Saved Backlinks](/docs/_assets/saved/bookmarked-backlinks.webp)

<Callout type="warning">
<p>Reorder and mark-done need a signed-in account. Anonymous bookmarks on the public hub stay in browser storage until you sign in — they can disappear if you clear the browser.</p>
</Callout>

## Mark outreach done

Check **Done** when you finish work on a site. Clear **Done** to return it to your active list.

Click **Save Shortlists** after you reorder or change done flags so OpenQuok stores your list on your account.

## Storage and cookies

Hub picks may live in browser storage until you sign in. OpenQuok uses first-party cookies for account features. Clearing site storage for OpenQuok can remove unsynced Build Backlinks picks on that browser. See the <a href="/cookie-policy">Cookie Policy</a>.
